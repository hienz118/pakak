What is Caraga?

Caraga is the Philippines' Region XIII, tucked into the northeastern shoulder of Mindanao where the mountains meet the Pacific. Its regional center, Butuan City, is one of the oldest settlements in the country but most travelers know Caraga for what's outside the city: Siargao's surf towns, Surigao del Sur's curtain-wide waterfalls, the wetlands of Agusan Marsh, and quiet island provinces still figuring out tourism on their own terms. It's a real, working region plan around ferry schedules and rainy months, and it rewards you generously.

This guide sticks to five provinces and thirteen spots we'd actually bring a family to, each one noted with an honest entrance fee, best season, and a plain read on how kid-friendly it is.
